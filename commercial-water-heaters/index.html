Symptoms That Mean It's Time to Call

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you money and keep a slow drip from turning into a flooded utility room. These are the red flags our technicians see most often before a breakdown:

  • A commercial heater that has grown noticeably louder
  • Scalding bursts or wild temperature swings at showers or sinks
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank
  • Equipment approaching the end of its expected service life
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • The tank giving up in the middle of a rush
  • Trouble reaching sanitation temperatures in the kitchen
  • Long waits for hot water at sinks far from the heater

How to Prevent Water Heater Problems

A little routine care goes a long way toward keeping a water heater running well past its expected lifespan. Follow these tips from our technicians:

  • Schedule upgrades during slow periods instead of waiting for a breakdown
  • Verify delivery temperatures at sinks and showers a few times a year
  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast
  • Record fixture temperatures regularly, especially before inspections
  • Replace consumed anode rods before the tank itself starts to corrode
  • Keep the area around the heaters clear for combustion air and service access
  • Keep recirculation pumps and check valves in service to avoid long waits and wasted energy
