Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Few businesses work a water heater harder than a restaurant. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and a high-temperature dish machine needs a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Livonia Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we plan visits around your operating hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and log every visit in written reports that inspectors and landlords can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, so hot water is ready when the first shift arrives.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Livonia Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Livonia and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is pinpointing the fault and restoring hot water fast, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we set up a maintenance schedule so it doesn't happen again, and every response comes with full documentation for your records.

Preventive Care

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Our commercial maintenance program is built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include tank flushing and anode checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at key fixtures. Hard water shortens the life of commercial equipment quickly, so high-use properties usually need more frequent visits.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to talk through a plan sized to your property and budget.
